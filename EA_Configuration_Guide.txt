💰 POSITION SIZE CALCULATION (Lot Size Formula)
================================================================================

Formula:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Account Balance = $10,000
Risk % = 15%
Risk Amount = $10,000 × 15% = $1,500

SL Distance = 150 pips
Entry Price = 2450
SL Price = 2400
Risk Pips = 2450 - 2400 = 50 pips ❌ (ไม่ใช่ 150)

ถ้า SL_Buffer = 150:
SL Price = 2415 (Previous Low) - 150 pips = 2265
Risk Pips = 2450 - 2265 = 185 pips ✅

Lot Size = Risk Amount / (Risk Pips × Pip Value)
         = $1,500 / (185 × 10)
         = $1,500 / $1,850
         = 0.81 lots

ปรับเป็น 0.80 lots (ปัดลงตามมาตรฐาน)

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

⚠️ ข้อสังเกต:
- หากคำนวณ Lot Size < 0.01 EA จะไม่เปิดออเดอร์
- วิธีแก้: เพิ่ม Account หรือลด SL_Buffer

🔍 TREND FILTER EXPLANATION (HH/HL Check)
================================================================================

Higher High / Higher Low (Uptrend):
───────────────────────────────────

H4:
  Bar 1: High=2400 Low=2350
  Bar 0: High=2450 Low=2380
  ✅ 2450 > 2400 (HH) AND 2380 > 2350 (HL) = UPTREND

H1: (Same Logic)
  ✅ HH/HL = UPTREND

M30: (Same Logic)
  ✅ HH/HL = UPTREND

Result: ✅✅✅ ALL 3 CONFIRM = Buy Signal Ready


Lower Low / Lower High (Downtrend):
───────────────────────────────────

H4:
  Bar 1: High=2500 Low=2450
  Bar 0: High=2480 Low=2420
  ✅ 2480 < 2500 (LH) AND 2420 < 2450 (LL) = DOWNTREND

Result: ✅ Sell Signal Ready

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ความสำคัญ:
- Trend ต้องอยู่ใน 3 TF: H4, H1, M30
- ไม่ได้ใช้ Moving Average (EMA) - ใช้ Price Action เท่านั้น
- Trend ต้องต่อเนื่อง ไม่ใช่ One-off Candle
